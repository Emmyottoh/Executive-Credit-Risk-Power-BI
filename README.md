# Executive Credit Risk & Borrower Default Analytics — Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard developed to analyze borrower credit risk, loan default patterns, credit score distributions, and financial indicators.

The project examines 500 applicant records, presenting executive-level insights to support credit risk assessment, borrower profiling, and informed lending decisions.

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Applicants:** 500
- **Default Rate:** 8.4%
- **Average Credit Score:** 654.7
- **Total Compensation:** $13.92M
- **Average Compensation:** $27.84K

## Technical Stack & Tools

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and business intelligence reporting
- **Data Visualization:** Executive KPI cards, comparative charts, and financial risk reporting
- **Credit Risk Analytics:** Borrower profiling, credit score evaluation, and default analysis
- **Business Intelligence:** Translating applicant-level data into decision-support insights

## Analytical Objectives

- Evaluate loan default patterns across applicant profiles.
- Examine credit score distributions and borrower risk characteristics.
- Analyze compensation indicators in relation to credit risk.
- Present key lending risk indicators through executive reporting.
- Support informed credit assessment and portfolio monitoring.

## Business Value

The dashboard consolidates important borrower risk indicators into an accessible analytical view, helping financial analysts and lending decision-makers identify potential risk patterns and assess applicant characteristics.
